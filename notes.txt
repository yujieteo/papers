https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26269, Scholze's paper on six functor formalism. Key definitions include: (1) Corr(C,E) to Cat_infty as a lax symmetric monoidal functor with right adjoints to projection formula, resulting in proper base change; (2) straightening and unstraighting resulting in the definition of a symmetric monoidal infinity category. Computations include: (1) Kunneth proof on page 8 to help define projection formula; (2) trivial Kan fibrations to define infinity categories on page 14; (3) propositional definitions 3.11 and 3.12 for Mann's definition resulting in a computation of the projection formula on pages 23 and 24.

https://mgallauer.warwick.ac.uk/docs/m6ff.pdf, Gallauer's six functor formalisms. Key definitions: (1) cohomology and homology in terms of six functors on the tensor unit with a dictionary in page 8; (2) Grothdieck duality dictionary on page 10; (3) relative purity for smoothness on page 11; (4) definition of localisation on page 15; (5) definition of proper and smooth base change on page 16; (6) definition of coefficient systems and axioms on page 18. Computations: (1) computation of a smooth and blow up triangle as an example on page 16;

https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Gestalten.pdf, Scholze's paper on Gestalten or the opposite category of Stefanish rings. Key definitions: (1) from pages 11 to 12 the buildup to the definition of a Stefanich ring is given. Key examples: (1) examples of Tannakian duality are given on page 6 to page 8.

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.12456, Khan's notes on stacks. Key definitions: (1) simplicial sets on page 8 as well as the horn filling condition leading to infinity categories from page 8 to page 11; (2) sheaves and stacks from page 14 to 16. Key computations: (1) working out explicitly what nerves are in Remark 1.2.2. concretely; (2) working out concretely the descent condition based on totalisation criterion on pages 20 - 21.

https://terrytao.wordpress.com/books/additive-combinatorics/, Terence Tao and Van Vu's book on Additive Combinatorics: Key definitions are: (1) first moment method by Markov's inequality; Key lemmas are: (1) Markov's inequality; (2) union bound by linearity of expectations; (3) Borel-Cantelli lemma by probability as expectation of indicators, monotone convergence theorems, and Markov's inequality; (4) exercise on Bonferroni's inequality by inclusion and exclusion principle inductively after taking expectation of indicators and applying the union bound; (5) popularity principle by taking expectations on both sides.
